# Knowlumo CSCA Mathematics — Season 1 episode plan

**Working production plan.** Season 1 contains **12 long lessons**, each about five minutes, plus matching vertical Shorts/Reels cutdowns. This is our teaching plan, not an official CSCA episode list, not a release schedule, not a promise that a topic appears on an exam, and not an official weighting. Requirements may change; check the current official syllabus and the intended university's rules.

The earlier `sources/Youtube-arena-01b47ded-youtube/csca/CURRICULUM.md` contains a 48-item detailed curriculum map. That map remains an internal long-range reference for later seasons; it is **not** the public Season 1 count.

## Module 1 — Sets and inequalities

1. **Sets Made Simple** — elements, membership, Venn diagrams, intersection, union, empty set, original practice question. *Built and verified.*
2. **Subsets & Complements** — subset, proper subset, universal set, complement, set difference, Venn regions, original practice question. *Five-minute draft built; not published.*
3. **Intervals & Inequalities** — set-builder notation, interval notation, number lines, inequality rules, original practice question.

## Module 2 — Functions

4. **Functions: Inputs & Outputs** — definition, notation, mappings, one-output rule.
5. **Domain & Range** — allowed inputs, resulting outputs, interval answers.
6. **Graphs & Transformations** — reading graphs, shifts, reflections, even/odd symmetry.

## Module 3 — Geometry and algebra

7. **Coordinates & Lines** — distance, midpoint, slope, line equations, parallel and perpendicular cases.
8. **Circles & Conics** — circle equation plus an overview of ellipse, hyperbola, and parabola graphs.
9. **Vectors & Complex Numbers** — coordinates, vector operations, dot product, imaginary unit, conjugates, modulus.

## Module 4 — Probability and statistics

10. **Probability Basics** — outcomes, events, equally likely cases, complementary events.
11. **Mean, Variance & Spread** — interpreting data, mean, variance, standard deviation.
12. **Mixed Practice & Common Mistakes** — timed mixed questions across all four modules, error review, final recap.

## Format locked for every episode

- 1280x720, true 30 FPS, H.264/AAC, English narration.
- About five minutes of real explanation: definition, visual example, rule, original practice question, think pause, delayed answer, recap.
- Dynamic subtitles reveal words only as spoken, hold the last group during pauses, and do not cover formulas.
- Bright repeatable 16:9 thumbnail with Knowlumo avatar, episode number, short topic title.
- Matching 1080x1920 / 30 FPS Shorts/Reels cutdown with 2–3 questions, think pauses, delayed answers, safe UI layout, and a final card using the real thumbnail.
- Separate source and result files; build scripts, timings, and metadata committed.
- No invented URL, no clickable-card claim, no official-CSCA claim, no promise that a practice question appears on the exam, no fabricated dates, metrics, contact details, or finished courses.
